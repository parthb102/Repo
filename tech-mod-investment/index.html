Why this is defensible — assumptions in plain English

Benchmark basis & framework notes
  • 6Rs disposition framework (retire, retain, rehost, replatform, repurchase, refactor/rewrite) is the standard Gartner/cloud-migration taxonomy for portfolio modernization — the same approach described in the QB/EPA thread.
  • Per-app unit costs reflect typical fully-loaded ranges: retire/decommission $0.1–0.2M; rehost (lift-and-shift) $0.2–0.4M; replatform $0.5–1.0M; repurchase-to-SaaS $1–2M (license + implementation + data + change); refactor/rewrite $2–5M blended across size. Tune per discovery.
  • Investment-to-savings multiples of ~2–3× annual run-rate are consistent with the 2–3 year simple paybacks underwritten on large efficiency-led IT transformations.
  • Reinvestment share of ~10–18% of annual IT budget into modernization is an elevated "change-the-bank" allocation sustained only during active transformation.
  • GenAI uplift on eligible modernization work (code understanding, test generation, refactoring, migration) — model conservatively; benefit concentrates on rewrite/replatform, not the full estate.
  • Gross vs net: gross is the full program cost; net deducts capacity freed by sunsetting non-target apps (redeployed to target-state build) and run-rate savings banked in-flight. The investment committee ask is the net.

Ranges are typical industry orders of magnitude for structuring the ask, not a substitute for portfolio discovery. Replace unit costs and mix with client-specific data from the application-mapping exercise (dependencies, integrations, LoC/complexity sizing) as it lands.
